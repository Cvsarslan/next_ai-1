Our Story

From a Single Office to a Regional Leader

Founded in 1999 in Dubai, Apex Ledger & Audit began as a boutique accounting practice with a single partner and a commitment to delivering honest, meticulous financial services. Over two and a half decades, we have grown into a full-service CPA firm with offices across the UAE, serving over 500 clients from SMEs to publicly listed corporations.

Our growth has always been driven by one thing: the unwavering trust of our clients. We believe that great accounting is not just about numbers — it is about understanding the unique story behind every business and helping it write a better future.
